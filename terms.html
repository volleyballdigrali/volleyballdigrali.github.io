By using it you confirm that you have any permission required from your club and from players' parents or guardians to record this information, and that you will follow your club's, league's and school's policies and the law.

Use the information only for coaching your team, share files only with people who need them, and delete information you no longer need. Never post sync or setup files anywhere public.

When scouting other teams, record players by number only, not by name.
